## Institutional rules (apply to everyone)
- **Minimum length: 40 pages** (HND), EXCLUDING preliminary pages, references and appendices. Below 40 = not accepted for assessment/defense.
- **Font:** Times New Roman, 12 pt. **Spacing:** double line. Standard institutional margins. Headings consistent.
- **Referencing:** APA, 6th edition, strictly.
- **Plagiarism:** must not exceed 30% on approved detection tools.
- **AI-generated content:** must not exceed 30% of the project writing; the submitted work must reflect the student's own intellectual effort. → This is why **I write the paper myself**; the build work and complete tracking live in track.md, and any draft produced along the way is purely material for me to rewrite in my own words — I will not submit text I didn't write.
- **Supervisor:** work goes to the assigned supervisor for vetting/proofreading; **no defense without the supervisor's written approval**; final submission needs supervisor signature; hard + soft copies.
- **Defense:** physical, in-person, held annually **October–November**.
- Content must be thoroughly developed — superficial treatment risks rejection/revision.
- Final binding: hardbound.

### A. School of Engineering Technology
Prelims: Title Page, Certification, Dedication, Acknowledgements, Abstract, Table of Contents, List of Figures, List of Tables, List of Plates (if applicable), List of Symbols/Abbreviations
- Ch 1 Introduction: 1.1 Background · 1.2 Statement of the Problem · 1.3 Aim and Objectives · 1.4 Significance · 1.5 Scope · 1.6 Definition of Terms
- Ch 2 Literature Review: 2.1 Conceptual Framework · 2.2 Review of Related Engineering Systems/Technologies · 2.3 Theoretical Framework · 2.4 Review of Similar Projects/Designs · 2.5 Gap Identification
- Ch 3 Methodology / System Design: 3.1 System Overview · 3.2 Design Specifications · 3.3 Materials/Components Used · 3.4 Circuit/System Design (Block Diagram) · 3.5 Working Principle · 3.6 Tools/Software Used
- Ch 4 Implementation, Testing and Results: 4.1 Construction/Development Process · 4.2 Testing Procedures · 4.3 Results and Observations · 4.4 Performance Analysis · 4.5 Discussion of Results
- Ch 5 Conclusion and Recommendations: 5.1 Summary of Work · 5.2 Conclusion · 5.3 Recommendations · 5.4 Suggestions for Further Work · 5.5 Limitations
- Back matter: References (APA 6th), Appendices (drawings, code, datasheets)

### B. School of Industrial and Applied Sciences
Prelims: same as Engineering (may include List of Equations)
- Ch 1 Introduction: 1.1 Background of Study · 1.2 Research Problem · 1.3 Aim and Objectives · 1.4 Research Questions/Hypotheses · 1.5 Significance · 1.6 Scope of Study
- Ch 2 Literature Review: 2.1 Conceptual Review · 2.2 Theoretical Framework · 2.3 Empirical Studies · 2.4 Gap in Literature
- Ch 3 Methodology: 3.1 Research Design · 3.2 Study Area · 3.3 Population and Sample · 3.4 Data Collection Methods (Lab/Field) · 3.5 Experimental Procedures · 3.6 Data Analysis Techniques
- Ch 4 Results and Discussion: 4.1 Data Presentation (Tables/Graphs) · 4.2 Analysis of Results · 4.3 Interpretation · 4.4 Discussion in Relation to Literature
- Ch 5 Conclusion and Recommendations: 5.1 Summary · 5.2 Conclusion · 5.3 Recommendations · 5.4 Contribution to Knowledge · 5.5 Limitations
- Back matter: References, Appendices (raw data, lab results)
